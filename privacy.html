Privacy

How LoKo.i handles the information you share with us.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: your name, contact details, trip or availability details, interests and languages. We do not use tracking cookies or ads.

Why we use it

To connect travelers with locals, contact you about a meetup, keep everyone safe, and improve LoKo.i using anonymous statistics.

Who sees it

The LoKo.i team, and only the matched local or traveler for your meetup, who sees just what is needed to meet. We never sell your information.

Where and how long

Stored in Google Sheets (Google LLC, servers outside Korea) for 1 year from submission, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time. Contact us at Instagram @loko.i.kr.

Who is responsible

LoKo.i, Busan, Korea. Privacy contact: Kim Minwoo, founder.
