Walks & nature

Busan is a city of coastlines and hills. A local knows the quiet stretches and the best time of day.

  • Igidae Coastal Walk이기대 해안산책로

    Go late afternoon for Gwangan Bridge views across the water.

  • Haeundae Blueline Park trail해운대 블루라인파크 산책로

    Walk Mipo to Cheongsapo along the old rail line, then take the hill road for the view.

  • Jangsan장산

    An easy local hike above Haeundae. Wear real shoes; the top is rocky.

Sports

The easiest way to feel like a local: move together.

  • Beach run해운대·광안리 아침 러닝

    Locals run the sand path early in the morning, before the crowds.

  • Songjeong surfing송정 해변 서핑

    The calmest beach for first-timers, with surf shops right on the sand.

  • Futsal pickup game풋살

    Your local can book an indoor court. Bring indoor shoes.

Games

Busan fans are famous for their energy. Learn the cheers from someone who grew up with them.

  • Lotte Giants baseball사직야구장 롯데 자이언츠

    Season runs spring to autumn. Your local will teach you the chants.

  • Busan KCC Egis basketball부산 KCC 이지스 농구

    The winter option: indoor, loud and close to the court.

  • Busan IPark football부산 아이파크 축구

    A relaxed local match, easy to join on the day.

Food & markets

Eat where locals eat, and order like one.

  • Dwaeji gukbap돼지국밥

    Busan's pork soup. Your local will show you how to season it at the table.

  • Gukje & Bupyeong markets국제시장·부평깡통시장

    Go in the evening for the night market stalls.

  • Jeonpo café street전포카페거리

    Small independent cafés, a short walk from Seomyeon.

Culture & art

Hillside villages, old factories turned art spaces, and the stories behind them.

  • Gamcheon Culture Village감천문화마을

    Go early before the tour groups, and wander the back alleys.

  • Huinnyeoul Culture Village흰여울문화마을

    A cliffside lane on Yeongdo with sea views at every turn.

  • F1963F1963

    A former wire factory, now a bookstore, gallery and garden.

By the sea

Busan from the water and from above.

  • Sky Capsule & Beach Train스카이캡슐·해변열차

    Book ahead on weekends. Sunset rides sell out first.

  • Songdo Marine Cable Car송도해상케이블카

    Pick a glass-floor cabin if you are brave.

  • Gwangalli at night광안리 야경

    On many Saturday nights there is a drone light show. Check the schedule first.
