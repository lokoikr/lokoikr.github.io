This is what your local will see

Your email and contact ID stay private until you both say yes.

Personal information (required)
  • We collect: name, country, age range, email, contact ID, trip dates, group details (incl. kids' ages), activities, area, time, languages, buddy preference, a line about you and notes. Optional: first visit, Korean level, food budget, your photo.
  • Why: to connect you with a local, contact you about the meetup, keep everyone safe, and improve LoKo.i (statistics are anonymous).
  • How long: 1 year from submission, or deleted right away when you ask.
  • Where: stored in Google Sheets and Google Drive (Google LLC, servers outside Korea).
Sharing with your matched local (required)
  • Shared with: the ID-checked LoKo.i local you are matched with.
  • What: first name, photo, country, age range, your intro, group size, activity, date, time, area, languages and notes. Your contact ID is shared only after you both say yes.
  • Why: to plan and meet on the day. We ask locals to delete it within 7 days after the meetup.
Safety rules (required)
  • Meet only in public places, and wrap up by 10 pm.
  • No home or hotel visits, and no rides in private cars.
  • Not a dating app. Unwanted advances get you removed.
  • Everyone pays for their own food, tickets and transport.
  • Locals are not paid guides. LoKo.i connects people and is not a party to the activity, but we act on every report right away.
